      Tunnel parts

        How it goes together

        1. On the bench: fan 1 onto your adapter with its four fan screws from the plate’s front, as it is now.
        2. On the bench: rail frame on fan 2’s back, then fan 2 and the straightener. Push the four pins in from the rail frame side until their arrowheads poke out of the straightener, then press the lot onto fan 1’s back until all four click.
        3. Front panel off: hang the stack on two fan screws through the middle fan’s top holes, from the outside. Leave them loose.
        4. On the bench: plug the EPS lead into the card, then push the duct’s sleeve onto the card end until the plate inside sits on the card’s end strip. It’s a snug push fit, like the v9 test piece.
        5. Still on the bench: drive the card’s three end screws through the plate from inside the duct, with a long screwdriver through the duct’s inlet. B and C go straight in; A, at the tray end, takes the screwdriver tilted a little toward the middle.
        6. Lay the case on its side and lower the card into its slot. The duct’s flange drops into the rail frame from the glass side and slides down with the card; the rail’s lead-in lifts or lowers the loose stack into line.
        7. Snug the two front screws last, just until their heads touch the steel; don’t pull the stack forward. The lead lies in the trough and sweeps up over the stack; park the EPS adapter on top and plug in the two PCIe leads.

        Check when the duct is done

        1. Snap off the supports: inside the sleeve under the screw plate and the trough’s climb, and now also under the two fills. They’re the same thin breakaway kind as on the test pieces.duct v10 · about 16 h 07 min · 179 g · started 16 Nov 23:52
        2. Push the duct onto the card. It should go on just like the v9 test piece; the fills sit in front of the end strip and don’t touch the card.fit surfaces exactly v9 · fills 0.5 mm off the strip, 1.2 mm off the board
        3. Screw it on through the plate: B and C straight in, A with the screwdriver tilted a little toward the middle. The fills leave each screw room even at either end of its slot.slots 5.6 × 3.6 mm, ±1 mm play · 4 mm clear round each screw
        4. The card’s end screws. The plate adds 2 mm under each head, so if the ones from the card come up short, use ones 2 mm longer, probably M3.model: 2 mm plate
        5. The pins: two came out clean from the four-pin plate, and two more were printed one at a time. Push each through fan 2 and a straightener corner into fan 1; its arrowhead should click just behind fan 1’s back flange, like the test pin did.pin 1 · 0.30 mm barb · holes 4.4, and 4.6 through the straightener
        6. The tray side. Your adapter already fits there, and the rail frame and the duct’s flange are no wider, 124 mm, so they should clear too.model: 2 mm
        7. The height. With the card in, the flange should enter the rail without forcing. If it doesn’t, loosen the two screws and let the stack slide in its slots.model: case holes 3 mm below the slots’ centres, ±4 to spare
        8. The flange should slide in by hand and sit flat on the rail frame.model: 0.3 mm dovetail clearance
        9. The trough’s end should just meet the card end round the plug.model: 0.1 mm
        10. First power-on only with the EPS plug in and both fans running. Watch the card’s temperature with nvidia-smi.
