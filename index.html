What the model shows

  • The case has four bay positions. Bay 1, at the bottom, lines up with PCIEX16_2, and bay 2 with PCIEX16_1.
  • With the cages in bays 3 and 4, bays 1 and 2 are clear, about 155 mm. That fits the 2 + 2 fan duct on either slot, or a dual duct for two cards.
  • The two middle fans sit 20 mm apart with a printed straightener between them. Two fans pressed together reach about 1.5 times the pressure of one; with the straightener it's closer to 1.8 times.
  • This plan assumes the bay 4 cage clears the radiator fans. If it doesn't, cages in bays 2 and 3 still leave bay 1 for a low duct.
  • PCIEX16_2 runs at PCIe 3.0 x8, and with two cards both slots run at x8. For inference that mostly means slower model loads.
  • Two V100s and the 9900K can draw about 750 W at full load from the 850 W PSU. Give each card its own power feed.
  • Each V100 covers the x1 slot just below its x16 slot. Two cards leave one empty slot between them.
